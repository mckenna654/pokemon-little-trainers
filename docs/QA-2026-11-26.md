# QA play-through and accessibility review: 26 November 2026

**Status, 26 November 2026:** QA-01 to QA-07 are fixed in `v0.1.0-dev.4` (patches 0039–0042;
see the end of this report). The findings below describe the build that was tested.

**Build:** repo `develop` at `9c8cf67` (game branch `3295c5b`), version 0.1.0-dev.3. ROM SHA-1
`7d7e33b779032bddfe3cdcaceb6a9c3b96b18314`, SHA-256
`0c7fa284158211a139bc5662df867309bbc5817d5f336195c5b0887d0d627491` (the released
`v0.1.0-dev.3`, rebuilt from clean). **Emulator:** headless mGBA 0.10.5 core only. Nothing here was
played by a person or a child, and no other emulator or device was used.

**Scope:** QA only. No gameplay, difficulty, experience or artwork was changed. Tests, test
tools and this report were added; one test-tool bug was fixed (QA-10).

## Summary

- **The main story can be finished with every starter.** From a fresh save, using only the arrow
  and the A button (for Bulbasaur and Charmander, also the direction presses that walk to their
  ball), Squirtle reached the Hall of Fame at 207 minutes of play, Charmander at 211 and
  Bulbasaur at 270. No memory was edited and no story flag was set.
- **The arrow held up under abuse.** It led on to the next goal in all 40 generated runs that
  wandered off at random or walked backwards along the path. All eight directions appeared, with
  clean pictures.
- **Random mashing never trapped the game or lost a Pokémon.** That held for 20 generated runs of
  150 presses of every button.
- **Two permanent-loss paths remain on Little Trainer:** throwing an item away (QA-01) and
  releasing a Pokémon, including the starter (QA-02). Pressing A alone cannot do either.
- **Pressing A alone can get stuck** after choosing CHANGE while trapped (QA-03). Brock's Onix
  knows Bind, so this can happen at the first gym.
- **An evolution that can never finish keeps starting** (QA-04). With Little Trainer's lower
  friendship threshold, Chansey and Golbat begin evolving after level-ups, and the game cancels
  every attempt.
- **Pacing depends heavily on the starter.** Bulbasaur lost to the Champion twice and had to beat
  the Elite Four three times; several leaders took far more than the documented 3–5 hits (S-01).
- **The post-game is out of reach on Little Trainer,** and after the Hall of Fame the arrow has
  nothing left to point at (D-03).
- **Most screens away from battles and the field are words only:** bag, PC, shop, party, Pokédex
  and options (D-05).

## Findings

Severity: **High** means a child can lose something for good or cannot go on without an adult.
**Medium** means a child is confused, stuck until they press a different button, or made to repeat
a long stretch. **Low** means it is visible but harmless. Modes: L = Little Trainer, J = Junior,
C = Classic.

### Confirmed bugs

#### QA-01 High (L, J): an item can be thrown away for good (issue #3)
- **Reproduce:** Start → BAG → choose an item (a Potion) → A → DOWN, DOWN (TOSS) → A → A (YES is
  already chosen).
- **Expected:** nothing is lost for good on Little Trainer (`docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md`, rule 3).
- **Observed:** "Threw away 1 POTION(s)." The item is gone. The Yes/No box has no tick or cross,
  and YES is chosen to start with.
- **Evidence:** `builds/qa/probe/toss-sheet.png`; `builds/screenshots/qa-toss.png`.
- **Frequency:** always.
- **Pressing A alone:** does not reach TOSS (A on an item chooses USE).
- **Suggested fix:** on Little Trainer, leave TOSS out of the bag's item menu, as CHANGE is left
  out when it cannot work.
- **Regression:** `emu_qa_flows.PermanentLoss.test_an_item_cannot_be_thrown_away` (expected
  failure until fixed).

#### QA-02 High (L, J): a Pokémon, including the starter, can be released for good (issue #4)
- **Reproduce:** at any Pokémon Centre PC, or by the test fixture that opens the PC: DEPOSIT
  POKéMON → the Pokémon → A → DOWN ×3 (RELEASE) → A → UP (YES) → A.
- **Observed:** "CHARMANDER was released." It is gone from the party and the boxes.
- **Evidence:** `builds/qa/probe/rel-sheet.png`, `rel-sheet2.png`; `builds/screenshots/qa-release.png`.
- **Frequency:** always.
- **Pressing A alone:** never releases anyone, because the question starts on NO. This is
  confirmed by `test_mashing_a_at_the_release_question_lets_nobody_go`: 30 presses of A at the
  release question. The same presses with UP first do release, so the question is reached.
- **Suggested fix:** on Little Trainer, leave RELEASE out of the PC's menus.
- **Regression:** `emu_qa_flows.PermanentLoss.test_a_pokemon_cannot_be_released` (expected
  failure).

#### QA-03 Medium (L): after CHANGE while trapped, pressing A alone goes round for ever (issue #5)
- **Reproduce:** trainer battle, a second Pokémon in the party, the lead held by Bind, Wrap or
  Mean Look → CHANGE → keep pressing A.
- **Observed:** the party screen starts on the other Pokémon. A → SHIFT → A → "CHARMANDER can't be
  switched out!" → A → back to the list, and round again. After 150 presses it is still in the
  party screen. B gets back to the battle; the trapped Pokémon stays in.
- **Evidence:** `builds/qa/probe/trap-sheet.png`.
- **Frequency:** always when trapped. Opponents that trap include Brock's Onix (Bind), wild
  Tentacool and Ekans, and Gastly/Haunter (Mean Look). The set-up writes the trap directly (labelled
  in the test); a trap in real play was not reproduced.
- **Suggested fix:** hide CHANGE while the Pokémon cannot be switched out (the same idea as hiding
  it when there is nobody to change to).
- **Regression:** `emu_qa_flows.Trapped.test_change_while_trapped_comes_back_with_a_alone`
  (expected failure); `..._b_gets_back_and_the_pokemon_stays_in` passes.

#### QA-04 Medium (L, J): an evolution that can never finish keeps starting (issue #6)
- **Note:** unmodified FireRed does the same at friendship 220, but rarely reaches it. Little
  Trainer's 150 makes it common.
- **Observed:** the late-game run shows "What? CHANSEY is evolving!" between Lance and the Champion
  (`builds/qa/journey-arrow-late/sheet-29.png`, row 1 column 2, `shot-0338.png`). Chansey stays
  Chansey.
- **Cause, from the source:**
  - Little Trainer and Junior lower the friendship needed to evolve to 150.
  - Chansey (into Blissey) and Golbat (into Crobat) reach it.
  - FireRed cancels evolutions into Pokémon beyond the first 151 until the National Pokédex
    (`src/evolution_scene.c`, "Automatically cancel...").
  - The National Pokédex is out of reach on Little Trainer (D-03).
- **Frequency:** after every level-up of a Chansey or Golbat with friendship of 150 or more. The
  late-game run shows it about 12 times between Fuchsia and the Hall of Fame
  (`builds/qa/journey-arrow-late/`, shots 69, 122, 181, 198, 257, 302, 316, 338, 427, 437, 450
  and 500). Each time there is the evolution's white-out and swirl, and nothing happens.
- **Reproduce:** `emu_qa_flows.Evolution`: a Chansey with friendship 160 and a Rare Candy. The
  evolution screens `CB2_BeginEvolutionScene` and `CB2_EvolutionSceneUpdate` open, and the
  species does not change.
- **Suggested fix:** in the friendship evolution check, skip targets the game would cancel
  anyway (species beyond 151 before the National Pokédex).
- **Regression:** `test_no_evolution_starts_that_the_game_will_stop` (expected failure).

#### QA-05 Low (L): running does not always work, although Little Trainer's rule says it should
- **Observed:** RUN from a wild Diglett, whose Arena Trap stops escape in the original game, fails
  on Little Trainer. Shadow Tag (Wobbuffet) and Magnet Pull work the same way, from the same check
  (`IsRunningFromBattleImpossible`). The battle carries on normally.
- **Expected:** the rule `alwaysRunAway` is described in `include/little_trainers.h` as "running from
  a wild Pokemon never fails". The README tested here (`9c8cf67`) also said running always works;
  the README rewrite of 26 November (`8a15f69`) no longer says so.
- **Frequency:** always against those abilities. Diglett's Cave is optional.
- **Suggested fix:** either let Little Trainer's always-run rule win over trapping abilities, or
  reword the rule's description.
- **Regression:** `emu_qa_flows.Trapped.test_run_from_a_pokemon_with_arena_trap` records the
  current behaviour.

#### QA-06 Low (L): CATCH is offered where it can never work
- **Observed:** in the Pokémon Tower Marowak battle the picture menu shows CATCH
  (`builds/qa/journey-arrow/shot-0643.png`). The game always dodges a ball thrown at the ghost
  (`battle_script_commands.c`, ghost branch of `Cmd_handleballthrow`), with words only.
- **Frequency:** once per game.
- **Suggested fix:** leave CATCH out in that battle.
- **Regression:** none yet; it needs the Tower state.

#### QA-07 Low (L): with the party and every PC box full, CATCH only plays a failure sound
- **Observed:** nothing on screen says why. The battle can still be won or run from, so it is not a
  trap.
- **Evidence:** `builds/screenshots/qa-catch-when-full.png`.
- **Frequency:** only with all 420 PC places full, which the test set-up fills directly.
- **Suggested fix:** leave CATCH out, or grey it, when there is nowhere to put a Pokémon.
- **Regression:** `emu_qa_flows.CatchingWhenFull` (passes; guards against a trap).

#### QA-08 Low (L, J): some Yes/No boxes have no tick and cross, and some start on YES
- **Observed:** the bag's TOSS, the shop's SELL and the PC's RELEASE use words-only YES/NO boxes.
  The field's own Yes/No box shows the tick and cross.
  - Toss and sell start on YES.
  - Release starts on NO.
- **Evidence:** `toss-sheet.png`, `rel-sheet2.png`, `builds/qa/scenarios/shop-*.png`.
- **Suggested fix:** use the same pictures everywhere. QA-01 and QA-02 would remove two of the three
  boxes.

#### QA-09 Low (L): the arrow is drawn over people and furniture next to the player
- **Observed:**
  - Over Lorelei, Bruno and Agatha when the player stands in front of them.
  - Over a Rocket Grunt (Rocket Hideout B1F).
  - Over a desk (Silph Co. 7F) and a computer (Sea Cottage).
  - Over Mum at the table.
- **Assessment:** it is always clear where it points. Cosmetic.
- **Suggested fix:** none needed. If wanted, draw the arrow behind people.

#### QA-10 (test tool, fixed in this branch): the harness could see an arrow that was not there
- **Observed:**
  - `FireRed.nav_arrow_shown()` trusted the sprite slot where the arrow last was.
  - After a map change that slot can briefly hold another sprite. Here it held one at x=904 with a
    different callback, and the helper reported "DOWN-RIGHT" with nothing on screen.
  - The game itself checks the sprite's template before reusing it, so players were never
    affected.
- **Fix:** the helper now checks the template too (`tests/emu/firered.py`).
- **Effect on earlier results:** the follow-the-arrow player could, for a frame, take its direction
  from the wrong sprite.
  - The three journeys, the late-game run and the generated sweep ran **before** this fix, and all
    finished.
  - The full suite was run again **after** it (see `docs/TESTING.md`).

### Suspected problems and pacing

- **S-01 Medium (L): difficulty depends heavily on the starter, and losing late means a long
  repeat.**
  - The turn counts are how often the battle buttons came up in the whole battle, across all of
    the leader's Pokémon. That is not the same as rule 4's "hits", but it is close enough to show
    the spread.
  - Some leaders fall below the 3–5 target too: Brock and Misty took 2 with Squirtle and
    Bulbasaur.
  - With Bulbasaur:
    - Erika took 13 turns and Blaine 15, against the target of 3–5.
    - The rival at Silph Co. won twice.
    - The Champion (whose Exeggutor resists Bulbasaur's attacks) won twice.
    - Each Champion loss sends the player back to redo the whole Elite Four: three times in all,
      about 70 minutes of play.
  - Koga took 9–10 turns with Squirtle and Bulbasaur.
  - In the resumed second half (`journey-arrow-late`), even Squirtle lost to the Champion once.
    - The battle lasted about 7.5 minutes (shots 339–405).
    - Venusaur repeats Sunny Day, Growth, Synthesis and SolarBeam, and the Champion uses a
      Sitrus Berry and two Full Restores.
    - The Elite Four then had to be beaten again.
    - The first Squirtle run won the same fight: the outcome turns on luck.
  - Charmander lost to Misty twice.
  - These are automated A-mashing results with one main attacker. A child might switch or catch
    more, or less.
  - **Suggestions:**
    - Fewer Pokémon for the Champion.
    - A Little Trainer rule that a Champion loss returns the player to the Champion's door.
    - Type-aware damage help for the starter that is weak against a leader.
- **S-02 Medium (L): an attack's picture is not stable.**
  - The left button always shows the generic burst. The right button shows the type of whichever
    move is judged stronger against the current opponent.
  - So Bite shows as the burst in one battle and the moon in the next, Water Gun moves between
    buttons, and Wing Attack shows as the burst and as the feather.
  - A child cannot learn "the drop is the water attack" when the drop moves or disappears.
  - Evidence (shots 741, 743, 771 and 887 of the arrow run; Charmander run shots 0100 and 0242):
    `builds/qa/journey-arrow/sheet-62.png`, `sheet-65.png`, `sheet-74.png`;
    `builds/qa/journey-charmander/sheet-09.png`, `sheet-21.png`.
  - **The ring stays on the button chosen last.** It starts on the suggested attack only at a
    battle's first menu. When the opponent changes, an A-masher keeps choosing the move it chose
    before, even when the star has moved to the other button: Blastoise used Tackle five times
    against Lapras instead of Hydro Pump (`journey-arrow-late` shots 418–423; also 264 and 406).
  - **Suggestion:** give each move its own type picture on both buttons, and keep the order fixed.
    Move the ring to the suggested attack whenever a new opponent comes out.
- **S-03 Low: unverified colour points.**
  - The Pokémon Tower 7F gravestones look gold where other floors are lilac
    (`builds/qa/journey-arrow/shot-0645.png`).
  - A full-white battle background during a send-out in the Tower (`shot-0594.png`).
  - Neither was compared with the unmodified game in the same place. In Brock's gym, the
    unmodified game and Little Trainer show the same white background and pink send-out flash.

### Deliberate choices that need a decision from Jake

- **D-01, saving (L):**
  - The game saves itself when a story goal is reached, not after a catch.
  - A Pokémon caught and not saved is lost if the power goes off
    (`emu_qa_flows.SavingAndModes.test_a_pokemon_caught_without_saving_is_lost_when_the_power_goes_off`).
  - Saving from the Start menu after four badges and reloading kept everything (place, party,
    badges, mode, money).
  - **Option:** also save after a catch or an evolution.
- **D-02, selling (L, J):** a shop's SELL turns items into money, with YES chosen to start with.
  Money has little use on Little Trainer. **Option:** leave SELL out on Little Trainer.
- **D-03, after the Hall of Fame (L):**
  - CONTINUE puts the player at home in Pallet Town with the game marked as cleared. No arrow
    shows, and the arrow has no goals left (`builds/qa/scenarios/post-game-*.png`).
  - Bill never offers the Sevii Islands trip on Little Trainer, and Cerulean Cave's guard leaves
    only after an event on One Island. So the Sevii Islands, Cerulean Cave, Mewtwo and the National
    Pokédex are out of reach unless MODE is switched.
  - **Options:** post-game goals for the arrow; or a "you did it!" screen and a clear end.
- **D-04, MODE (L):**
  - Anyone who opens OPTION can switch to Classic.
  - The arrow disappears at once and the help with it; switching back restores both
    (`qa_scenarios mode-switch`).
  - The mode is saved with the game.
  - Puzzles already opened on Little Trainer stay open.
  - Classic has no free Poké Ball.
- **D-05, words-only screens:**
  - Bag (pocket names, USE/GIVE/TOSS).
  - PC (all menus).
  - Shop (BUY/SELL and prices).
  - Party screen (SUMMARY/SWITCH/ITEM, SEND OUT, SHIFT), which also shows HP as numbers, not
    hearts.
  - Options, level-up stat boxes, lift floor lists, the Pokédex, and the Old Man's catching
    demonstration.
  - Pressing A gets through every one of them on the main route. They are where a child who cannot
    read needs an adult.
- **D-06, story content a parent may want to know about:**
  - Pokémon Tower: a graveyard, ghosts, possessed Channelers, and the ghost of a Marowak killed by
    Team Rocket.
  - Team Rocket as a criminal gang, holding Silph Co.'s staff hostage.
  - The Game Corner's slot machines.
  - "Player defeated…", the original text that uses "Player" instead of the child's name.
  - Strong full-screen animations, such as Psychic in Saffron Gym.
- **D-07, catching:** an A-masher never catches anything before the Safari Zone, because the
  cursor starts on an attack. The Squirtle run had one Pokémon for 143 minutes, and the game was
  still finishable with it. Catching is the child's choice, as the design intends.

### Investigated and not defects

These were raised by reviewers or tests and each was checked:

- **"Broken arrow on the player's head" in caves:** it is the cave ladder the player arrives on. It
  stays put while the real arrow appears beside it (`builds/qa/probe/cave-trace.png`). The
  "striped balls" are the same ladders elsewhere.
- **"No arrow" in the Safari Zone and other places:** the pictures were taken the instant the player
  could move. The arrow appears 0 to 27 frames (under half a second) after arriving, the Safari
  Zone included. In the Safari Zone it showed 57 to 250 times per map.
- **"Badges show no badge":** the journey pictures were taken after the leader's speech. In a real
  gym the confetti falls during the speech (28 pieces at its peak,
  `builds/qa/probe/badge-real-60.png`).
- **Tour pictures:**
  - Hearts jumping in the forced leader fight (the tour writes HP, as its code says).
  - The player named JAK in one part and JANNE in another (two separate runs).
  - The `PKMN` glyph and "Player defeated" (original FireRed).
- **Mid-typing text, fade frames, send-out flashes and stat-change colours:** original behaviour or
  frames caught mid-animation.

## Areas tested and not tested

**Tested (automated, headless mGBA):**
- The main story, all three starters.
- Wandering off the arrow, and walking back along it into earlier maps, from 10 points across the
  game.
- Talking to people and defeated trainers while wandering.
- Random mashing of every button with random hold lengths.
- All eight arrow directions.
- Catching, missing and catching again; full party; full PC.
- Trapped switching and running.
- Bag toss, PC release, shop sell, party screen.
- Saving and reloading after story progress, and after an optional catch.
- Mode changes and their persistence.
- The post-game start.

**Not tested:**
- Play by a person or a child.
- Other emulators and real hardware; sound, timing and feel.
- A whole game on Junior or Classic.
- Bag pockets other than Items.
- Buying in shops.
- The Pokédex screens.
- The Game Corner, Power Plant, Seafoam Islands and legendary birds.
- The Sevii Islands (unreachable on Little Trainer).
- Losing on purpose at every gym.
- A trap set by an opponent's real move (the trap was set up directly).
- The two colour points in S-03 against the original game.

The generated runs started from every third goal save state (goal000, 003, 006, 009, 012, 015,
018, 021, 024 and 028; the numbering skips goals that changed within one step) with seeds 1 and 2. Other points and seeds were
not run. Every generated run records its seed, start state and actions.
- A failure would have been shrunk and saved to `builds/qa/property/failures/`, with the ROM's
  and state's SHA-1. There were none.
- `--replay FILE` repeats a saved failure.
- The shrinker only accepts the same failure, makes at most 150 replays, and is unit-tested
  (`tests/test_qa_shrink.py`).
